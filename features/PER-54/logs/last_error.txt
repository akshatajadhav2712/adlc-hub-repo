PER-54.1 is implemented. The GraphQL service now keeps an in-memory todo list and still returns `{"status": "ok"}` from `health`.

`addTodo` stores a UUID, the title, and `done: not_done`. An empty title raises `Title cannot be empty` and does not store a todo. `todos` returns the current list. `completeTodo` sets `done` to `done`, or raises `Todo not found` for an unknown id. The list lives in `todos_store` and is cleared on process start.
